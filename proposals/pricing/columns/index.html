Questions

  • What if the scope changes?

    We agree the scope on the first day. If something new comes up, we price it before doing it, so the date and the price you agreed stay as they are.

  • How do payments work?

    Half when we start and half on launch. Monthly is paid at the start of each month.

  • Can we pause Monthly?

    Yes. Tell us before the next month starts and we stop. Pick it up again whenever you need us.
