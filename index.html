FAQ

  • What does a project cost?

    Most projects fit one of three fixed prices: Launch at €19,900, Polish at €6,900, or Monthly at €4,900 a month. If yours does not, we price it after a 30-minute call, before any work starts.

  • How soon can you start?

    Usually within two weeks of the first call. We take on a few projects at a time, so each one gets our full attention.

  • Who will we work with?

    The three of us on this page, and no one else. One of us owns your project from the first call to launch and is the person you talk to.

  • Can you work with our existing brand?

    Yes. If the brand works, we build on it. If it holds you back, we tell you why and what we would change, and you decide.

  • Can you work with our developers?

    Yes. We can ship changes in your codebase, or hand over designs and components your team can build from without guessing.

  • What do we need to prepare?

    Not much. Tell us what you are making, who it is for and what has to be true on launch day. We write the words too, if you need us to.

  • What happens after launch?

    Everything we make is yours: the code, the files and the accounts. Your team can run it without us. If you want us to stay on, Monthly keeps us on call.

  • Do you work with clients outside Europe?

    Yes. We work in English, mostly in writing, with calls in the hours Prague shares with your time zone.

  • Do you use AI?

    Yes, the way we use every good tool: to spend less time on production and more on decisions. Every decision still has one of us behind it.
